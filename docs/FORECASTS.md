# Reset-aware forecasts and safe burn rates

`src/forecast/engine.ts` forecasts each provider quota window from ledger provider snapshots (Prompt 2.1, operation key `2026-10-10.1/prompt-2.1`). It is a pure function over the ledger (see [LEDGER.md](LEDGER.md)) and writes nothing. The only unit is provider-reported quota percent; token estimates are never converted into quota.

Five-hour, weekly and daily windows are forecast independently (`provider + account alias + window`). The daemon exposes `GET /api/forecast?provider=&account=&window=&reserved=` (local only).

## Method

1. The latest window segment is selected with the ledger's reset rules (moved `resetAt`, or a drop larger than 1 point). Earlier segments are ignored and flagged `reset_in_history`.
2. Readings with a duplicate or backwards effective time are dropped (`clock_anomaly_dropped`). A small downward correction keeps usage monotonic instead of creating negative burn.
3. Burn rate is the distribution of pairwise slopes between readings at least 5 minutes apart (Theil-Sen style). Slopes beyond 3.5 robust standard deviations are removed (`outliers_removed`). The reported range is the 10th to 90th percentile, the point is the median, in points per hour.
4. **Safe hourly allowance** = `(100 - reserved - used) / hours to reset`, never negative.
5. **Projected limit time** is a range `[remaining / high burn, remaining / low burn]` and is shown only when it falls before the reset. `latest: null` means the lowest plausible burn never reaches the limit. It is displayed as a point only when the burn spread (`high / low`) is at most 2 and there are at least 8 readings; otherwise `display: "range"` with `wide_burn_spread` or `sparse_history`.
6. **Probability of exhaustion before reset** is the share of plausible burn rates that would reach 100 percent by the reset, smoothed and clamped to 0.01-0.99. Only an observed provider cap reports 1 (`state: limited`).
7. **Fallback**: with fewer than 4 readings or under 20 minutes of history, the estimate is usage divided by elapsed time in the window (`method: window_average`), always low confidence, always a range. With no reset time and too little history there is no forecast (`insufficient_data`).
8. **Stale data** (last reading older than 30 min for five-hour, 6 h weekly, 2 h daily, or its window already reset) returns `state: stale` and no limit time or probability.

## Safeguards against repeated or impossible 100 percent forecasts

- Uncapped windows never report probability 1; the ceiling is 0.99.
- A projected limit time earlier than the latest reading is impossible by construction (it is `now + remaining / rate`), and a limit after the reset is dropped.
- `riskNotice(prev, next)` surfaces a high-risk forecast once, then only when the risk class changes or the earliest limit time moves by more than 30 minutes.

## Observability

The route logs `state`, `method` and `reasons` for each request. Every forecast carries `reasons[]`, `method`, `confidence` and a plain-language `explanation`.

## Backtest

`src/forecast/backtest.ts` replays each snapshot as a forecast origin using only readings up to that time, and compares the predicted usage at 1, 3, 6 and 24 hours with the (interpolated) later reading in the same window. It reports mean absolute error in quota points per provider, window and horizon against a stay-flat baseline, plus a Brier score and calibration bins for the exhaustion probability.

### Known limitation: exhaustion probability is not calibrated

On the same fixtures the Brier score is 0.133 (claude five-hour, 402 forecasts). High-probability bins over-forecast: the 0.8-1.0 bin averaged 0.92 forecast probability but 9 percent of those windows were actually exhausted; the 0.6-0.8 bin averaged 0.71 against 18 percent. The probability extrapolates the current burn to the reset and does not model bursty, idle gaps. Until it is recalibrated against real beta history, user interfaces must present it as risk ("likely", "unlikely"), not as a percentage, and must not gate behaviour on a specific value. Recalibration is open work for the Phase 2 gate evidence (model card, backtests on real history).

## Out of scope for this prompt

Recommendations (2.2), UI (2.3) and decision alerts (2.4). Local activity records are not used as burn evidence because their units (estimated tokens) differ from quota percent.
