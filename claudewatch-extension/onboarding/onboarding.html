Why Token Watcher asks for each permission

Nothing below reads your messages. Each permission is used only for the purpose listed.

Access to claude.ai, chatgpt.com and gemini.google.com
Lets the extension read token counts and quota figures from these three sites. It is not used on any other site.
cookies
Lets the extension make quota requests to your signed-in provider account without asking you to paste a key. Cookie values are never stored or sent anywhere.
storage
Keeps your settings and usage history on this device.
alarms and idle
Schedules periodic refreshes and pauses them while your computer is idle.
notifications
Shows an alert when usage crosses a threshold you chose.
tabs and scripting
Finds your open provider tabs and runs the usage gauge in them.
localhost:7734 (optional)
Only requested if you turn on sharing with Token Watcher Core running on your own computer.

Read the full privacy page

🔒 Privacy: Your usage data stays on your device. Token Watcher reads token counts and quota figures only; it never reads message content and has no analytics or tracking servers. It talks only to the provider sites you use (and to localhost if you opt in).
